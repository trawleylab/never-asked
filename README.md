# The Never-Asked — ADC 2026 poster (paper 880)

Landing page for poster 880, IDF-WPR & ADC & Metabolic Diseases Congress 2026,
Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre, 19–21 August 2026. Poster board 141.

Hare K, McAuley S, Trawley S. *The Never-Asked: Understanding the Gap in Emotional
Health Support for People Living with Diabetes in Australia.*

**Live at <https://trawleylab.github.io/never-asked/>** — this is the address encoded
in the QR code printed on the poster.

| File | What it is |

## Changing anything here

The QR code on the printed poster encodes the URL above, not any particular file.
The page can therefore be edited freely — adding the DOI once the paper is published,
for instance — without reprinting the poster. Two things must not change:

- the repo name, and
- the GitHub Pages setting (deploy from `main`, root folder).

Either would break every printed QR code.

If `poster_880.pdf` is regenerated, replace it here under the same filename so the
download link on the page keeps working.

## Provenance

Every figure on the poster and page traces to the analysis in the project's `FINAL/`
folder — `dm_revision_results.txt`, `Table2_Logistic_Regression.docx` and Tables 1–2
of the thesis draft. Headline figures are the unweighted estimates, matching the
accepted congress abstract.

Language throughout follows the Diabetes Australia *Our Language Matters* position
statement (2021).
